Selected publications

Representative publications highlighting the major research directions presented on this site.

Structural regulation and molecular recognition

  • You Y, Katti S, Yu B, Igumenova TI, Das J.
    Probing the diacylglycerol binding site of presynaptic Munc13-1.
    Biochemistry (2021), 60(16), 1286–1298.
    Integrated NMR, mutagenesis, cellular assays, docking, and molecular dynamics to define structural determinants of ligand binding and membrane recruitment in Munc13-1.

  • Neupane NP†, Mathews JT†, Lingaraju S†, You Y†, et al.
    Mitragynine and Morphine Stabilize Different Conformations of the µ-Opioid Receptor as Revealed by Molecular Dynamics Simulation Studies.
    ACS Omega (2026).
    Applied molecular docking and molecular dynamics to examine ligand-dependent conformational states of a neuronal signaling receptor.

Structural proteomics and protein interactions

  • Madison HJ, Dunn L, You Y, et al.
    Oligomeric assemblies of plant biotin carboxylase revealed by cryo-EM and cross-linking.
    Biochemical Journal (2026), 483, 803–817.
    Integrated XL-MS restraints with structural analysis to support higher-order models of biotin carboxylase assembly.

  • He Y, Song H, You Y, et al.
    A model of human APOA2 on HDL.
    Journal of Lipid Research (2026), 67(9), 101113.
    Used XL-MS-derived restraints to support structural modeling of APOA2 and its interactions with APOA1 on HDL.

Post-translational modification biology

  • You Y†, Tsai C-F†, Patel R, et al.
    Analysis of a macrophage carbamylated proteome reveals a function in post-translational modification crosstalk.
    Cell Communication and Signaling (2023), 21, 241.
    Integrated carbamylation, acetylation, and phosphorylation proteomics to investigate PTM crosstalk and the functional relationship between carbamylation and ubiquitin regulation.

  • You Y, Sarkar S, Deiter C, et al.
    Reduction of Chemokine CXCL9 Expression by Omega-3 Fatty Acids via ADP-Ribosylhydrolase ARH3 in MIN6 Insulin-Producing Cells.
    Proteomics (2025), e202400053.
    Combined cell-based perturbation, quantitative proteomics, and functional analysis to investigate ARH3-dependent regulation of inflammatory CXCL9 signaling in pancreatic β cells.
